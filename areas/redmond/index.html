Selling in Redmond

Redmond has slowed down: the median home took 28 days to sell from July to September 2026, 14 days longer than a year ago, and there are about 3.1 months of supply. See the full Redmond numbers. Market figures are for single family homes from July to September 2026, from the Redfin Data Center, and are updated monthly.

Only 93 single family homes sold in Redmond from July to September 2026. With that few sales spread across Education Hill, Grass Lawn, the Overlake area and the plateau toward Redmond Ridge, the comparables you choose do a lot of the work in setting the price.

I price off the sales that actually happened near you, fix only what buyers will notice, and stay on top of the listing once it's live. Here's how I sell homes, or get a price range for yours.

How I work in Redmond, with recent sales

Redmond homes range widely in price, so the valuation has to hold up.
