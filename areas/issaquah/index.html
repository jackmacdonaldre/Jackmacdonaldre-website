Issaquah Real Estate

Issaquah sits at the edge of the Cascade foothills along I 90, with a historic downtown, the Issaquah Highlands, and hillside neighborhoods on Squak and Cougar Mountain. Trails start practically in town.

I've sold Issaquah homes and condos and helped buyers purchase here, from a Sycamore single family home to condos in Sammamish Hills, Sammamish Pointe and Hamptons Pointe.
