Bothell Real Estate

Bothell straddles the King and Snohomish county line along I 405, with a rebuilt downtown on the Sammamish River, the UW Bothell campus, and established neighborhoods like Canyon Park and North Creek. Most of it is in the Northshore School District.

Latest market report · Updated October 2026

Bothell's median single family sale price was $1.12 million for July to September 2026, down 5.9% from the same months last year. 98 single family homes sold (18.1% more than a year ago), and the median home spent 28 days on the market.
