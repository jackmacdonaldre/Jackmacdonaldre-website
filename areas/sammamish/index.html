Sammamish Real Estate

Sammamish is a plateau of mostly single family neighborhoods between Lake Sammamish and the foothills, served by both the Lake Washington and Issaquah school districts. Lots tend to be larger than in the closer in cities.

Latest market report · Updated October 2026

Sammamish's median single family sale price was $1.60 million for July to September 2026, down 3.1% from the same months last year. 158 single family homes sold (13.2% fewer than a year ago), and the median home spent 24 days on the market.

Selling in Sammamish

Sammamish homes are selling a little faster than last year: 24 days at the median from July to September 2026, with sellers getting about 96.6% of list price on average. See the full Sammamish numbers.

Sammamish had about 3.7 months of supply from July to September 2026, the most of the five Eastside cities I track most closely. When buyers have more homes to choose from, condition and presentation carry more weight.
