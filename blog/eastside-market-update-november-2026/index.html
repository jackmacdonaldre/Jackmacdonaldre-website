Eastside Market Update: November 2026

By Jack Macdonald
Macdonald Group of Compass

Here's where the Bellevue, Redmond, Kirkland, Issaquah, Sammamish, Woodinville, Bothell, Kenmore, Newcastle, Renton, North Bend, Fall City, Carnation and Shoreline housing markets stand for August to October 2026. Every number below is for single family homes over a rolling 3 month window, from the Redfin Data Center.

Bellevue

Bellevue single family homes sold at a median price of $1.72 million over the August through October 2026 period, down 2.9% from $1.77 million the same stretch last year. Fewer homes traded hands too, 253 sales compared to 314 a year ago, a drop of 19.4%. Supply has grown to 3.0 months and the median time on market has stretched to 27 days, up from 8 days a year ago.

  • Median sale price: $1,724,017 (same months last year: $1,775,000)
  • Homes sold: 253 (last year: 314)
  • Median days on market: 27
  • Average sale to list price: 96.9%
  • Months of supply: 3.0
