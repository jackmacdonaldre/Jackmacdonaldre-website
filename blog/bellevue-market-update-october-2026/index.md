Bellevue's median single family sale price was $1.76 million for June to August 2026, down 7.6% from the same months last year. 307 single family homes sold (9.7% fewer than a year ago), and the median home spent 16 days on the market.

## The numbers for June to August 2026

* Median single family sale price: $1,763,966 (same months last year: $1,910,000)

* Homes sold: 307 (last year: 340)

* Median days on market: 16

* Average sale to list price: 97.8%

* Months of supply: 2.7

These cover single family homes sold over a rolling 3 month window, from the Redfin Data Center.

## If you're selling

At 2.7 months of supply, buyers have some choice but not a lot. Homes that are prepared and priced right still move; homes priced on hope tend to sit. Sellers averaged 97.8% of list price, so buyers are negotiating. Pricing right from day one protects you from a string of reductions. Homes are taking longer to sell than a year ago (16 days at the median versus 7), so build that into your timeline.

## If you're buying

At 2.7 months of supply you have some room to look. That's more choice than a year ago, but well priced homes in the best locations draw competition. Homes averaged 97.8% of list price, so there is room to negotiate, especially on homes that have been on the market a while. Prices are softer than a year ago, which is worth knowing when you write an offer.
