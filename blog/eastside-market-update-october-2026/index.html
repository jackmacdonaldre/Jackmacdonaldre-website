Eastside Market Update: October 2026

By Jack Macdonald
Macdonald Group of Compass

Here's where the Bellevue, Redmond, Kirkland, Issaquah, North Bend and Fall City housing markets stand for July to September 2026. Every number below is for single family homes over a rolling 3 month window, from the Redfin Data Center.

Bellevue

Bellevue's median single family sale price was $1.84 million for July to September 2026, up 2.3% from the same months last year. 266 single family homes sold (15.6% fewer than a year ago), and the median home spent 20 days on the market.

  • Median sale price: $1,841,766 (same months last year: $1,799,950)
  • Homes sold: 266 (last year: 315)
  • Median days on market: 20
  • Average sale to list price: 97.4%
  • Months of supply: 3.1

Common Questions

What are median home prices on the Eastside right now?

For single family homes sold July to September 2026, according to Redfin Data Center figures: Bellevue $1,841,766; Redmond $1,541,885; Kirkland $1,374,452; Issaquah $1,635,598; North Bend $1,299,482; Fall City $732,208.

Is the Eastside a buyer's or seller's market?

It depends on the city. Months of supply for July to September 2026: Bellevue 3.1; Redmond 3.1; Kirkland 3.2; Issaquah 2.9; North Bend 1.9; Fall City 2.7. Under about 4 months still leans toward sellers, but pricing and condition decide how any one home does.
