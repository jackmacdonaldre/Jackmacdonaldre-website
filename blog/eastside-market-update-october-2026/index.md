Here's where the Bellevue, Redmond, Kirkland, Issaquah, North Bend and Fall City housing markets stand for July to September 2026. Every number below is for single family homes over a rolling 3 month window, from the Redfin Data Center.

Bellevue's median single family sale price was $1.84 million for July to September 2026, up 2.3% from the same months last year. 266 single family homes sold (15.6% fewer than a year ago), and the median home spent 20 days on the market.

* Median sale price: $1,841,766 (same months last year: $1,799,950)

* Homes sold: 266 (last year: 315)

* Median days on market: 20

* Average sale to list price: 97.4%
