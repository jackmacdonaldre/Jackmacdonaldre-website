Redmond Market Update: October 2026

By Jack Macdonald
Macdonald Group of Compass

Redmond's median single family sale price was $1.50 million for June to August 2026, down 5.0% from the same months last year. 102 single family homes sold (15.0% fewer than a year ago), and the median home spent 21 days on the market.

The numbers for June to August 2026

  • Median single family sale price: $1,499,122 (same months last year: $1,577,500)
  • Homes sold: 102 (last year: 120)
  • Median days on market: 21
  • Average sale to list price: 98.0%
  • Months of supply: 2.4

These cover single family homes sold over a rolling 3 month window, from the Redfin Data Center.

If you're selling

At 2.4 months of supply, buyers have some choice but not a lot. Homes that are prepared and priced right still move; homes priced on hope tend to sit. Sellers averaged 98.0% of list price. Most homes are selling close to asking, which tells me the pricing, not the market, decides the result. Homes are taking longer to sell than a year ago (21 days at the median versus 10), so build that into your timeline.

If you're buying

At 2.4 months of supply you have some room to look. That's more choice than a year ago, but well priced homes in the best locations draw competition. Homes averaged 98.0% of list price, so there is usually a little room to negotiate, but not much on the best homes. Prices are softer than a year ago, which is worth knowing when you write an offer.
