Northshore School District: What Buyers in Bothell and Woodinville Should Know

By Jack Macdonald
Macdonald Group of Compass

One of the first questions I get from families buying in Bothell or Woodinville is some version of: "What's the school situation here?" And it's worth a real answer, because Northshore School District is the kind of district where the details actually matter. The overall ratings are strong, but which school your kids attend depends entirely on where you buy, and those boundaries don't always line up the way people expect.

The Short Version on Northshore

Northshore School District covers a large footprint across Bothell, Woodinville, Kenmore, and parts of Kirkland and Redmond. It's consistently one of the higher performing districts in King and Snohomish counties, and that reputation is one reason homes in these areas hold value the way they do. But "Northshore" isn't one school experience. It's a collection of schools with meaningfully different enrollment sizes, program offerings, and community feels depending on where you land.

The district runs 20 elementary schools, six middle schools, and four high schools. Most buyers I work with are primarily thinking about high school, but the elementary and middle school assignments matter too, especially if you're buying a house you plan to stay in for ten or more years.

High Schools: The Four You'll Encounter

Northshore's four high schools are Bothell High, Inglemoor High, Woodinville High, and North Creek High. If you're buying in this area and you care about schools, these are worth understanding individually, not just as a group.

Bothell High School is one of the larger high schools in the district and draws from much of central and western Bothell. It has strong athletics and a broad range of programs. Families who have gone there tend to be proud of it, and it consistently posts solid graduation and college enrollment numbers.

Inglemoor High School sits in Kenmore but serves parts of Bothell. It's known for having strong academic programs and tends to draw a lot of attention from families who are comparison shopping between Northshore and Lake Washington School District to the south. If you're buying on the Bothell side near the Kenmore border, you may end up here.

Woodinville High School serves much of the Woodinville area and has a reputation for strong academics and a tight knit community feel given the more suburban, lower density nature of that part of the district. The school consistently performs well, and families in Woodinville tend to be very engaged.

North Creek High School is the newest of the four, opened in 2017 to relieve overcrowding across the district. It serves the Mill Creek adjacent areas and parts of Bothell north of the main corridors. Because it's newer, some buyers ask whether it's "as good" as the others. From what I've seen, it's well regarded and fully established at this point. The newer facilities are a real plus.

The Boundary Question Nobody Thinks to Ask Until It's Too Late

Here's the thing about school boundaries in Northshore: they can shift. The district has adjusted boundaries multiple times over the years, especially around North Creek as enrollment balanced out after it opened. Buying a home assuming your kids will attend a specific school is a reasonable goal, but you should verify the current boundary directly with the district before you close, not just rely on what a listing says or what a neighbor tells you.

The Northshore School District website has an Address Look Up Tool where you can enter an address and get the current assignment. Use it. And if you're buying new construction in a growing area, ask explicitly whether the school assignment is locked in or subject to change as the development fills out.

I've seen buyers fall in love with a house partly because of the assumed school assignment, only to discover after moving in that the boundary had shifted in the most recent redistricting cycle. It's not common, but it happens enough that it's worth the two minutes to check.

Programs That Cross Boundaries

Northshore has a few specialized programs that draw students from across the district rather than just from a specific attendance zone. The most notable is the district's highly capable cohort program, which groups advanced learners together at designated schools. If your child qualifies for highly capable services, their school placement may not follow the standard neighborhood assignment.

There are also choice transfer options within the district, though those come with no transportation guarantee and are approved on a space available basis. Families sometimes pursue transfers to a specific elementary school for a particular program or teacher reputation, but it's not something you can count on when making a buying decision.

How Schools Affect Price in This Area

Northshore's reputation does get priced into homes, especially at the elementary level where parents feel the most day to day connection to the school their kid attends. I've seen streets just one or two blocks apart carry noticeably different interest levels based on which elementary school they feed into, even when the homes themselves are nearly identical.

Woodinville in particular tends to price at a slight premium compared to equivalent homes in parts of Bothell, and the school district perception is one factor in that, alongside the lower density feel and larger lots. It's not the only factor, but it's real.

If you're buying primarily for school access and you're working with a tight budget, it's worth looking carefully at where you land in the boundary map rather than assuming any address in Bothell or Woodinville gets you the same school outcome.

What the District Doesn't Control

A district's quality can tell you a lot, but it doesn't tell you everything about whether a school is the right fit for your specific kid. Class sizes, the individual principal, extracurricular offerings, and the parent community at a given school can vary quite a bit even within a strong district. If school fit is a major priority for your family, visiting the actual school before you buy is worth doing, not just reading the ratings.

GreatSchools ratings are a starting point, but they weight test scores heavily and don't capture things like how a school handles kids who learn differently, the strength of its arts or technical programs, or how the staff communicates with families. Talk to parents in the neighborhood if you get the chance. That's usually more useful than any ranking.

Snohomish vs. King County and the District Overlap

Northshore straddles the King and Snohomish county line, which is one of the things that makes it a little unusual. Parts of Bothell are in King County, parts are in Snohomish County, and the school district assignment doesn't follow that county line at all. This matters practically because property taxes, county services, and some administrative things differ by county even if the school is the same. Buyers sometimes assume the county determines the school district or vice versa. They're independent of each other.

If you're buying in the canyon road corridors or the areas north of SR 522, pay attention to which county the property sits in. It affects your tax bill more than your school assignment does.

A Few Things Worth Verifying Before You Buy

  • Confirm the school assignment directly through the Northshore district's Address Look Up Tool, not through the listing agent or Zillow.
  • If highly capable or special education programs matter to your family, call the district's student services office and ask specifically how placement works, since it often doesn't follow neighborhood boundaries.
  • For new construction, ask the builder whether boundary decisions for that development have been finalized with the district.
  • Check when the last boundary adjustment happened in the area you're considering and whether another is being discussed.

Northshore is a genuinely good district and that's not just a talking point. But it's a big, diverse district with meaningful differences between schools and areas, and the families who research it carefully before buying tend to end up happier with their choice than those who assume it all works out the same regardless of address.

Common Questions

Does the whole city of Bothell fall under Northshore School District?

Most of Bothell is served by Northshore, but not all of it. A small portion of Bothell falls within the Edmonds School District, particularly in areas near the western edges of the city. Always verify the specific address rather than assuming the city name equals the district.

Which high school serves most of Woodinville?

The majority of Woodinville feeds into Woodinville High School, but parts of the area closer to the Bothell border may be assigned to Bothell High or North Creek High. Use the district's Address Look Up Tool to confirm before making a decision based on a specific school.

Is North Creek High School newer and does that affect its quality?

North Creek opened in 2017 and was built specifically to reduce overcrowding across the district. Being newer means the facilities are excellent. In terms of academic performance and community feel, it's considered on par with the other Northshore high schools at this point, though it has had less time to build traditions and alumni networks.

Can I request a transfer to a different school within Northshore?

Northshore does have an intradistrict transfer process, but approvals are based on available space and are not guaranteed. If a specific school is critical to your decision, buying within that school's attendance boundary is the only reliable way to secure that assignment.

How do Northshore home values compare to Lake Washington School District homes nearby?

Both districts are well regarded and both carry a premium compared to surrounding areas. Lake Washington tends to get slightly more attention from buyers focused on the Kirkland and Redmond markets, while Northshore is the primary driver in Bothell and Woodinville pricing. The gap between the two in terms of actual school outcomes is smaller than the difference in perception sometimes suggests.
