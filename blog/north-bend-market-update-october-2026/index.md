North Bend's median single family sale price was $1.29 million for June to August 2026, down 3.6% from the same months last year. 31 single family homes sold (11.4% fewer than a year ago), and the median home spent 9 days on the market. North Bend is a small market, so a few sales can move these numbers a lot.

## The numbers for June to August 2026

* Median single family sale price: $1,291,743 (same months last year: $1,339,500)

* Homes sold: 31 (last year: 35)

* Median days on market: 9

* Average sale to list price: 98.6%

* Months of supply: 2.0

These cover single family homes sold over a rolling 3 month window, from the Redfin Data Center.

## If you're selling

At 2.0 months of supply, buyers have some choice but not a lot. Homes that are prepared and priced right still move; homes priced on hope tend to sit. Sellers averaged 98.6% of list price. Most homes are selling close to asking, which tells me the pricing, not the market, decides the result. Homes are selling faster than a year ago (9 days at the median versus 14).

## If you're buying

At 2.0 months of supply you have some room to look. That's more choice than a year ago, but well priced homes in the best locations draw competition. Homes averaged 98.6% of list price, so there is usually a little room to negotiate, but not much on the best homes. Prices are softer than a year ago, which is worth knowing when you write an offer.
