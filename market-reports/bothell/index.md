# Bothell Home Values & Market Report | Updated November 2026

Web page: https://jackmacdonaldre.com/market-reports/bothell/
Updated: November 2026. Data window: August to October 2026 (rolling 3 months, single family homes). Source: Redfin Data Center.

Bothell single family home sales from August through October 2026 show a median sale price of $1.13 million, essentially flat from $1.14 million over the same 3 month stretch last year. Eighty one homes sold, nearly identical to the 82 sold a year ago, and homes averaged 33 days on market compared to 29 days last year. The bigger shift is in supply: months of supply has grown from 2.1 to 4.5, giving buyers noticeably more room than they had twelve months ago.

## What this means if you're selling

With homes selling at 98.8% of list price, serious buyers are still showing up, but they are taking longer to decide. Thirty three days on market versus 29 last year is not dramatic, but it reflects a more patient pool of buyers. Pricing accurately from the start matters more now than it did a year ago, when supply was much tighter.

## What this means if you're buying

Four and a half months of supply means you have real choices in Bothell right now, which is a different situation than the 2.1 months available a year ago. Sellers are landing about 98.8% of their asking price on average, so well priced homes are still moving, but you have more time to think and compare. Median prices have held steady around $1.13 million, so the market has not softened dramatically, it has just become more balanced.
