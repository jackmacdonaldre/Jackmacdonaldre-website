# Newcastle Home Values & Market Report | Updated November 2026

Web page: https://jackmacdonaldre.com/market-reports/newcastle/
Updated: November 2026. Data window: August to October 2026 (rolling 3 months, single family homes). Source: Redfin Data Center.

Newcastle single family home sales ran 27 transactions over the August through October 2026 period, down from 54 in the same 3 month window last year. The median sale price came in at $1.55 million, about 2.7% below the $1.59 million recorded a year ago. Homes averaged 35 days on market, and months of supply has grown to 3.1, up from 1.6 last year.

## What this means if you're selling

With twice the inventory relative to demand compared to a year ago, buyers have more options and less urgency, which shows up in that modest price dip. Homes are still selling close to list at 98.7%, so realistic pricing is getting rewarded. If you price to where the market actually is rather than where it was in 2025, you can still move a home in roughly 35 days.

## What this means if you're buying

Newcastle has shifted meaningfully toward buyers over this 3 month period, with 3.1 months of supply giving you more room to be selective than the 1.6 months buyers faced a year ago. Sellers are landing at 98.7% of list on average, so deep discounts are not the norm, but you are not fighting the same competition either. This is a good window to take your time and negotiate thoughtfully.
