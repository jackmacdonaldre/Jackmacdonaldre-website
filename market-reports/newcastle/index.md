## What this means if you're selling

At 2.6 months of supply, buyers have some choice but not a lot. Homes that are prepared and priced right still move; homes priced on hope tend to sit. Sellers averaged 97.6% of list price, so buyers are negotiating. Pricing right from day one protects you from a string of reductions. Homes are taking longer to sell than a year ago (29 days at the median versus 24), so build that into your timeline.

## What this means if you're buying

At 2.6 months of supply you have some room to look. That's more choice than a year ago, but well priced homes in the best locations draw competition. Homes averaged 97.6% of list price, so there is room to negotiate, especially on homes that have been on the market a while. Prices are softer than a year ago, which is worth knowing when you write an offer.
