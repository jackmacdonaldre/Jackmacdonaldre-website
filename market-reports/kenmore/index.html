Kenmore Home Values & Market Report

Kenmore single family homes sold at a median price of $1.09 million over the August to October 2026 period, down from $1.17 million during the same 3 month window last year. Forty three homes changed hands, compared to 59 a year ago, and supply has grown to 3.6 months from 2.3 months. Homes are still moving in a median of 20 days, and sellers are getting 98.2% of their list price on average.

What this means if you're selling

Prices in Kenmore are off about 6.8% from where they were a year ago, so setting the right list price matters more than it did. Buyers have more choices now, with 3.6 months of supply versus 2.3 months last fall, which means overpriced listings are easier to overlook. The good news is that homes priced well are still selling in around 20 days and landing close to full asking at 98.2% of list.

What this means if you're buying

More supply and softer prices give buyers in Kenmore a bit more room than they had a year ago. With 3.6 months of inventory, you are less likely to face the same urgency that defined this area in prior years. That said, well priced homes are still going under contract in about 20 days, so being prepared to move is still important.
