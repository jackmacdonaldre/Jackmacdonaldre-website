## What this means if you're selling

At 3.2 months of supply, buyers have some choice but not a lot. Homes that are prepared and priced right still move; homes priced on hope tend to sit. Sellers averaged 98.5% of list price. Most homes are selling close to asking, which tells me the pricing, not the market, decides the result. Homes are taking longer to sell than a year ago (22 days at the median versus 19), so build that into your timeline.

## What this means if you're buying

At 3.2 months of supply you have some room to look. That's more choice than a year ago, but well priced homes in the best locations draw competition. Homes averaged 98.5% of list price, so there is usually a little room to negotiate, but not much on the best homes. Prices are softer than a year ago, which is worth knowing when you write an offer.
