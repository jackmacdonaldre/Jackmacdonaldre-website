## What this means if you're selling

At 4.7 months of supply, this is closer to a balanced market. Buyers compare, so condition and price have to hold up next to the other homes they toured that weekend. Sellers averaged 99.1% of list price. Most homes are selling close to asking, which tells me the pricing, not the market, decides the result. Homes are taking longer to sell than a year ago (14 days at the median versus 8), so build that into your timeline.

## What this means if you're buying

At 4.7 months of supply you have real choices. Take the time to compare, and use inspection and price to negotiate where it's warranted. Homes averaged 99.1% of list price, so there is usually a little room to negotiate, but not much on the best homes. Prices are higher than a year ago, so waiting has not been cheaper lately.
