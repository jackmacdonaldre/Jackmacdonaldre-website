## What this means if you're selling

Homes that sold closed at 98.8% of list price on average, which means pricing accurately still matters. The median days on market was 18, up from 12 a year ago, so sellers should expect a little more patience from buyers before getting to the table. With only 20 sales over the 3 month window, one or two outlier sales can shift the median noticeably, so your specific street and condition will carry a lot of weight in how your home gets priced.

## What this means if you're buying

At 3.9 months of supply there is more inventory relative to demand than a year ago, which gives buyers a bit more room to breathe than they had in the same period last year. Homes are still closing near asking at 98.8%, so well priced homes are not sitting and waiting for lowball offers. The pool of recent sales is small at 20 homes, so comparable sales can be thin and appraisals and negotiations may feel less clear cut than in a higher volume market.
