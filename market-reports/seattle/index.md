## What this means if you're selling

At 2.7 months of supply, buyers have some choice but not a lot. Homes that are prepared and priced right still move; homes priced on hope tend to sit. Sellers averaged 100.2% of list price, so well priced homes are still getting full price or better.

## What this means if you're buying

At 2.7 months of supply you have some room to look. That's more choice than a year ago, but well priced homes in the best locations draw competition. Homes averaged 100.2% of list price, so plan on paying at least asking for the ones you really want. Prices are softer than a year ago, which is worth knowing when you write an offer.
