## What this means if you're selling

Sellers got 98.7% of list price on average, which means pricing accurately matters more than ever here. At 32 days on market, homes are not flying off the shelf, so patience and realistic expectations are part of the picture. The median sale price of $1.09 million is running below where it was a year ago, so sellers who anchor their price to older comps may find themselves sitting longer than they want.

## What this means if you're buying

With 2.3 months of supply and homes selling close to list at 98.7%, there is not a lot of room to lowball, but you are also not in a situation where everything is gone the first weekend. At 32 days on market on average, you generally have a little time to do your homework before making a move. Volume picked up meaningfully compared to last year, with 17 sales versus 11, so more activity is happening, though in a market this size that can reflect just a handful more transactions rather than a broad shift.
