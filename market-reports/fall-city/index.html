Fall City Home Values & Market Report

Fall City's median single family sale price was $732,000 for July to September 2026. 6 single family homes sold, and the median home spent 22 days on the market. With this few sales, one or two homes can swing the median a long way, so read it as a rough guide, not a trend.

What this means if you're selling

At 2.7 months of supply, buyers have some choice but not a lot. Homes that are prepared and priced right still move; homes priced on hope tend to sit. Sellers averaged 96.5% of list price, so buyers are negotiating. Pricing right from day one protects you from a string of reductions. Homes are taking longer to sell than a year ago (22 days at the median versus 15), so build that into your timeline.

What this means if you're buying

At 2.7 months of supply you have some room to look. That's less choice than a year ago, but well priced homes in the best locations draw competition. Homes averaged 96.5% of list price, so there is room to negotiate, especially on homes that have been on the market a while.
