Redmond Home Values & Market Report

Redmond single family homes sold at a median price of $1.52 million over the August to October 2026 period, down 10.3% from $1.70 million in the same 3 month window last year. Volume fell too, with 91 homes sold compared to 113 a year ago. Supply has grown to 3.0 months and the median days on market stretched to 28 days, up from 20 last year.

What this means if you're selling

A year ago Redmond sellers had 1.7 months of supply working in their favor; now there is 3.0 months of inventory, which means buyers have more choices and less urgency. Homes are still selling, but the average sale came in at 97.3% of list price, so aggressive pricing tends to leave money on the table rather than spark a bidding war. With 28 days as the median time to an accepted offer, sellers should expect to be patient and price carefully from day one.

What this means if you're buying

More supply and a 10.3% drop in median price compared to last year means Redmond is a more workable market for buyers than it was in 2025. At 3.0 months of supply you are not in a rush the way you would have been at 1.7 months, and the typical home is selling at 97.3% of list, so there is some room to negotiate. Twenty eight days on market as a median tells you most homes are not flying off in a weekend, which gives you time to do proper due diligence.
