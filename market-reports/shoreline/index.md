## What this means if you're selling

Prices are softer than they were a year ago, so sellers who bought on the assumption that last year's numbers would hold may need to recalibrate their expectations. At 3.6 months of supply, buyers have more options than they did at this time last year, which puts a little more weight on the buyer's side of the table. That said, homes that are priced right are still selling, and the 98.8% average sale to list ratio shows that well positioned listings are not giving away a lot at the negotiating stage.

## What this means if you're buying

With 3.6 months of supply and a median sale price of $805,000, Shoreline is a more balanced place to buy single family homes than it was a year ago when supply sat at just 1.7 months. Sellers are getting 98.8% of their list price on average, so there is not a lot of room to lowball, but you are also not in a situation where every offer needs to be significantly over asking. Homes are moving in a median of 14 days, so you still want to be prepared to act without dragging your feet.
