Updated: November 2026. Data window: August to October 2026 (rolling 3 months, single family homes). Source: Redfin Data Center.

Sammamish single family home sales from August through October 2026 show a median sale price of $1.48 million, down 10.2% from the same 3 month window last year when the median was $1.65 million. 155 homes sold over the period, compared to 173 a year ago. Supply has grown notably, with months of supply at 2.9 versus 1.3 last year, and homes are sitting about a month before selling.

## What this means if you're selling

Sellers in Sammamish are working with less leverage than they had a year ago. The average home is selling at 96.7% of list price, so pricing accurately from the start matters more than it used to. With 32 median days on market and more competition from other listings, a home that is overpriced will sit and go stale before buyers come back around.

## What this means if you're buying

Buyers have more room to work with here than they did a year ago. Supply at 2.9 months is still not a lot, but it is more than double what it was in the same period last year, which means you are less likely to be rushed into a decision. Homes are generally closing near 96.7% of list price, so there is some negotiating room, though well priced properties are still moving within about a month.
