Issaquah Home Values & Market Report

Issaquah's median single family sale price was $1.62 million for June to August 2026, up 8.0% from the same months last year. 70 single family homes sold (7.7% more than a year ago), and the median home spent 16 days on the market.

What this means if you're selling

At 2.5 months of supply, buyers have some choice but not a lot. Homes that are prepared and priced right still move; homes priced on hope tend to sit. Sellers averaged 98.0% of list price. Most homes are selling close to asking, which tells me the pricing, not the market, decides the result. Homes are taking longer to sell than a year ago (16 days at the median versus 9), so build that into your timeline.

What this means if you're buying

At 2.5 months of supply you have some room to look. That's more choice than a year ago, but well priced homes in the best locations draw competition. Homes averaged 98.0% of list price, so there is usually a little room to negotiate, but not much on the best homes. Prices are higher than a year ago, so waiting has not been cheaper lately.
