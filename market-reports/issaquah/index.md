## What this means if you're selling

Sellers are still getting strong prices here, with homes closing at an average of 98.3% of list price, which means buyers are not paying much above ask but sellers are not leaving much on the table either. Fourteen days to a sale is fast enough that well priced homes are not sitting. The uptick in supply to 3.1 months does give buyers a little more room to breathe than they had last year, so pricing carefully from the start matters more than it did in 2025.

## What this means if you're buying

With 3.1 months of supply on the market, Issaquah is not as tight as it was a year ago when supply sat at 2.0 months, and that extra inventory gives you more options and a bit more negotiating room. That said, homes are still moving in a median of 14 days, so you cannot take a slow approach if something fits. The median sale price of $1.62 million reflects where this market is sitting right now, and homes are generally closing very close to list, so lowball offers are unlikely to land.
