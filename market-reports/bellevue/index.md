Bellevue single family homes sold at a median price of $1.72 million over the August through October 2026 period, down 2.9% from $1.77 million the same stretch last year. Fewer homes traded hands too, 253 sales compared to 314 a year ago, a drop of 19.4%. Supply has grown to 3.0 months and the median time on market has stretched to 27 days, up from 8 days a year ago.

## What this means if you're selling

The days of a home going under contract in a week are largely behind us for now, with 27 median days on market telling you that buyers are taking their time. At 96.9% of list price on average, sellers are still getting close to asking, but pricing accurately from the start matters more than it did when supply was at 1.7 months. With 3.0 months of supply, buyers have real choices, so a home that is priced or presented poorly will sit while better positioned listings move.

## What this means if you're buying

Three months of supply gives you something you did not have a year ago: room to think. The median sale price of $1.72 million and an average sale to list ratio of 96.9% suggest that modest negotiation is possible, though sellers are not giving things away. With 27 median days on market, the urgency to write an offer the same afternoon you tour is largely gone, and that is a meaningful shift from the 8 day pace of a year ago.
