About

Jack Macdonald

REALTOR® · License #21022645
Macdonald Group of Compass
700 110th Ave NE, Ste 270, Bellevue, WA 98004

I'm a Bellevue native, born and raised here in the community I now get to serve as a REALTOR®. Over the past few years, I've been fortunate to become one of the top-producing agents in the greater Seattle area, but what I'm most proud of is the trust I've built with my clients.

I bring a tenacious work ethic and a balance of emotional intelligence and professional poise to every transaction. Clients often tell me they appreciate not just my market knowledge, but the way I communicate throughout the process and make them feel supported every step of the way. I know what it's like to be in their shoes — as both a homeowner and an income property owner myself.

Outside of real estate, I've always been passionate about soccer, fitness, golf, fishing, and staying active. Those parts of my life keep me grounded and remind me of the importance of discipline, teamwork, and consistency — qualities I bring to my work.
