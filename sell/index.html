How I sell your home

1. Price it off real sales

Pricing is where most listings go wrong. I build the price from homes that actually sold near you, not from what someone hopes to get, and I show you every number behind it.

2. Prep only what pays off

We walk the house together and pick the few things buyers will notice first: paint, lighting, landscaping, small repairs. I'll give you conservative cost ranges and the vendors my clients have liked. If something won't pay you back, we skip it.

3. Present it well

Professional photos, a clean listing write up and the right launch timing. Every detail gets looked at twice before we go live, because the first week on the market matters most.

4. Negotiate to protect your equity

Price is only part of an offer. I look at terms, contingencies, financing and timing, lay out the options and the risk of each, and you make the call.

5. Manage it through closing

Inspection, appraisal, escrow and every deadline in between. You'll hear from me before you have to ask.

Common questions

How do you decide on a list price?

I start with the homes that actually sold near you in the last few months, then adjust for condition, size, lot and updates. I'll walk you through every comp and show you exactly how I got to the number, so you're not taking it on faith.

Should I fix things up before listing?

Usually some, but not everything. I'll walk the house with you and point out the few things buyers will notice, along with rough costs. If something won't pay you back, I'll tell you to skip it.

How long will it take to sell?

It depends on your city, price range and the time of year. Each Eastside city has its own monthly market report on this site with current days on market, so we can look at real numbers for your area together.

What does it cost to sell?

Commission is negotiable and we'll go over it up front. I'll also give you a full estimate of your net, including excise tax, title and escrow, so there are no surprises at closing.

My home didn't sell with another agent. Can you help?

Yes. Once the listing has expired or been cancelled, I'm happy to look at what happened, from the price and photos to the timing, and give you an honest read on what I'd do differently.
